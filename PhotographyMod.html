Photography

This module is T003, Introduction to Photography which I had taken as a minor module during my third year in Republic Polytechnic.

When I first used Lightroom, I was surprised at how
powerful it was at editing photos. I enjoyed it greatly and will use it
if I ever plan on pursuing photography further.

I had prior experience working with photoshop
thanks to my design module. However, I was using features that I wasn't familiar with
I was surprised to find that it is about as good as lightroom for editing.

This was part of my graded assignment where we were tasked to advertise a lively clothing style that Rrepublic Polytechnic was coming up with and I went with the theme of punk since it is a type of style that I personally like and align with.

Alongside taking photos of the clothing, I also had to include an 'product' which I picked as the mask to be an accessory to this clothing style.
