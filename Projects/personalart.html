Personal Art

I'm a self taught artist who has been drawing since 2019, I have since drawn more frequently on digital programs. I mostly enjoy drawing fanart.

This is my main drawing program when I draw on my Ipad,
It includes features such as animation but I mainly just do illustrations on it.

Ever since I was gifted a trackpad for drawing,
I've been drawing on Ms Paint instead, I find the limited array of brushes can give way to
unique textures on the canvas.

Requested by a mobile game company, Mytona. They had asked me to draw their mascot Atlas, for one of their games, a hidden object game called Seekers Notes.

These two are sketches I had in mind for the main drawing above. I had sent both sketches to the team and they had decided on the first one. My inspiration mainly came from the character itself, whose name is Atlas. In the game, he's seen as a guiding light for the player so to combine the ideas I wanted to use the motif of the compass star you see in maps.

These are rendered drawings of a self insert character for Seekers Notes and one of the characters in the game called The Informant. I tend to do more experimental pieces with these two characters and also doing more abstract pieces.

These are other rendered drawings I've done for either myself or my friends. The ones with Informant were made based on his backstory in the game which is told mainly through text, so I wanted to give visuals to the most pivotal point in his story.

I used to make drawings traditionally, starting with a sketch, then lineart, colour and shading. However, more recently I've been making my sketches cleaner so that I can immediately colour on top of them.

The first 5 characters are from another game made by Mytona called 'Chef & Friends' in which players of the game were encouraged to draw fanart of the characters. My fanart ended up being featured on their facebook page. The other drawings were either for me or through an art trade with friends.

I wanted to include these last two drawings because I had taken photos of my friends and sketched them out using the photos as references. Occassionally when I struggle to draw something, I like to take reference photos myself as it makes it easier to see the form.
