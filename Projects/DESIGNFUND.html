Design

This Module is C257, Fundamentals of Design. We learnt Design principles and how to create a design document and some colour theory. We had to create a logo, namecard, word, sticker, and banner for our graded assignment.

This was my first time working with illustrator
and though it is a very powerful tool, I feel I wasn't given enough time
to fully grasp its features.

I had a easier time working with photoshop
and I quite enjoyed using it when working on my projects.

Here is a logo for my 'Brand' as we were told to make for our Graded Assignment. to show that i'm more based on design, i had a character that represents me holding a pencil. My initials are represented as part of the hair.

Here are other scrapped logos for this logo. I felt like they didn't convey the idea that i was going for as they didn't particularly represent me.

I liked the idea of having the front and back of the card be connected in some way. This namecard was more focused on the aspect of me as an artist and I quite like the use of my ponytail as a paintbrush.

Here are other designs I wanted for my namecard, I liked playing with the idea of music because that is something else that I'm interested in.

For this assignment, we were told to create a word uniquely to protray the type of person you are. As seen in the word, I am a musician, as I play the double bass. I used the C to represent the bass clef which i use to play. the note in the stanza is the A note in the bassclef, as a unique way to protray the letter A in 'musician'

These are the drafts I had for the word, the first idea was musician since I had the clearest vision for it, then I made a second draft for artist since I was an artist myself. The third image is the moodboard for those ideas.

For our Graded Assignment, we were told to create a brand for ourselves, and since I had chosen to go in the direction of artist, since I am an artist myself, I made these stickers to be relatable to artists.

We were told to create a banner that had a travel theme, along with using one design principle. Here, I used assymetry alongside movement with the large airplane with the photographs featuring various travel destinations.
