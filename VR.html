Unity Projects

This module is C337, Immersive Technologies, which I had taken as a module during my Second year in Republic Polytechnic. our lessons lead up to a Graded Assignment where we had to create a VR escape room using Unity.

This was my first experience with Unity and VR development.
I found it a bit confusing at first but over time as I was working on it more frequently,
I got more used to it, though I had to look up a couple videos to assist me.

The first step when planning the escape room, I had to come up with a theme in which I wanted something that had a cute exterior but had a dark undertone if you looked closer. This was the intial idea of a cute girl's room

In the story of my escape room, These girls were part of the hint for one of the puzzles in the game. In the context of the story in the escape room however, they were supposed to be girls that the owner of the room stalked. I wanted to make them have separate aesthetics with differing colour schemes to assist in the puzzle

These are screenshots from the escape room. It came out different from my initial design but it still holds up the design of a girl's room. I had modelled the pink shelf, the red closet, and the corkboard holding the photos myself.

Here is a demonstration of how the escape room plays out.

As a side class assignment, we were told to create a simple namecard so we can put a code on it such that when we scanned it in the unity camera, it would reveal a 3D model that we created in a AR space. So I went with a meat theme.

This is a demonstration of the namecard working in action. I modelled a little rack with meat products on it.
