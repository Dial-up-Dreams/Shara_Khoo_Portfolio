Final Year Project: ThinkAble

For my Final Year Project, we were to create a web application for a company that works with children to young adults with special needs. The application allows for the special needs people to do worksheets digitally, and for parents and teachers to monitor their progress with the different topics provided by the teachers. It also allows for teachers to create and send out worksheets to the parents as well as handling a subscription to unlock more worksheets for the parents.

I made all of these pages using Figma
which I had been familiar with through one of my modules which handled
UI/UX for applications. So I wasn't a stranger to this design tool.

I was in charge of handling the page for the teachers/admins so that they can create worksheets. these are the CRUD (Create, Read, Update and Delete) pages for the worksheets.

I was also in charge of creating the teacher/admin pages for the monitoring of students so here are the CRUD (Create/Read/Update/Delete) for the students.
